About me

Irupé Perea
“The camera comes along with me wherever I go — a show here, a plant there. Photos taken out there.”

I'm Irupé Perea, an Argentine photographer. My work moves between two worlds that are really one: live music — concerts, clinics, artists on and off stage — and still lifes, where light does the slow work on things that don't move.

Under the name Fotos Por Ahí I gather what I shoot along the way: dark stages and bright beams, plants against black, portraits taken mid-song.
